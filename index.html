Hello visitor!

I am Akshara Viswanathan. I come from the beautiful costal city of Chennai, India, and found my way into astronomy through a bachelor in engineering in India, a masters in astrophysics in the UK, and a PhD in the Netherlands. Currently, I live and do research in Paris, France (my 7th country of astronomy research). These days I study some of the Milky Way’s oldest stars and the streams left behind as smaller galaxies joined it. I want to use them to learn more about the very first stars that formed in the Universe and the nature of the mysterious dark matter. I like turning huge space-based and ground-based telescope catalogues and new observation I make from telescopes around the world into stories about how our Galaxy formed.

PhD Thesis

Mapping the ancient Milky Way through metal-poor stars

My PhD thesis (supervised by the amazing Else Starkenburg, and Amina Helmi) uncovers the Milky Way’s history through its oldest stars: mapping the inner and outer halo, tracing stellar streams and merger remnants, and charting the proto-Galaxy’s gradual transition into an ordered, rotating disc.

The thesis cover is a Marauder’s Map of the Milky Way, with its evolutionary stages arranged in time around an artistic Galaxy. My thesis defense day was one of the most emotional and beautiful days of my life! I wore an Indian saree with all my research projects painted on it together with a Milky Way, stellar streams, and my favourite Gaia satellite!

In my thesis, I also drew a water-colour based Galactic archaeology comic as a "Summary for non-experts", for my 3.5-year old nephew to understand my thesis. I had a lot of fun with it! Hopefully, I continue drawing more science comics.
